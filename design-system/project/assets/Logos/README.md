# Logos

From Canva's "Vertui" folder: the ring mark from `Vertui Horticulture.ai`, and the lockups cropped from pages 2 and 5 of `Vertui Horticulture-Brandidentity (1).pdf`. All are preview resolution (about 450px or smaller) on solid grounds, not transparent; replace them with full-size transparent exports before production use.

- `vertui-mark.png` — the ring alone, full colour on white. Favicons, avatars, small spaces.
- `vertui-lockup.png` — main logo, full colour on white: wordmark in `blue-lobelia` and `jadestone`.
- `vertui-lockup-reversed.png` — for `blue-lobelia` grounds: wordmark in `poisonous-ice-cream`, ring in colour.
- `vertui-lockup-white.png` — single-ink white version on `blue-lobelia`.
