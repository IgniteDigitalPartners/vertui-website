# FeatureCard

A white rounded card with an icon tile, bold title and short paragraph, from the Elementor image boxes on the home and Why Vertui pages.

**Consumer provides:** `div.vt-feature` containing `div.vt-feature__icon` (one single-ink SVG, filled `jadestone`), `h3.vt-feature__title`, and one `p.vt-body`.

- `radius-15`, `shadow-card` at rest and `shadow-card-hover` on hover; padding `space-32` × `space-20`.
- Lay cards out in a grid with `space-40` gaps, on `white` or `mist` sections.
- Titles are benefits in title case ("Use Less, Grow More"); keep body copy to two sentences.
