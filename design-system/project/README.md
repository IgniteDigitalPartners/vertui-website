Vertui Horticulture sells super-concentrated biological plant treatments to home gardeners, landscapers and growers in Canada and the US. The brand reads as science-backed but friendly: fresh greens and limes, a confident teal, deep blue ink, Poppins throughout.

## Content fundamentals

- **Voice.** Plain, encouraging and outcome-first. Speak to the reader as "you" and the brand as "we" / "Vertui". Short claims, then one sentence of proof.
  - "Maximize Soil Health, Naturally."
  - "Healthy Plants Start Below the Surface."
  - "Use Less, Grow More. Treat large areas with a small amount."
  - "Elevate your plants and protect the planet with eco-conscious solutions rooted in science and innovation."
- **Headings are title case**; eyebrows and hero headlines are uppercase ("WHY CHOOSE VERTUI?", "PRECISION TREATMENTS FOR LUSH, HEALTHY LANDSCAPES"). Buttons are capitalised verbs: "Learn More", "Shop Now", "Join the Waitlist", "Contact a Specialist".
- **Product pages follow one script**, in this order: Overview, Key Benefits, "Struggling with …?", "How <Product> Works", "When to Use <Product>", growth stage, coverage, application, FAQ, safety. Keep that order and those headings for new products.
- **Name products in full**, title case, every time: Root Ignite Bio, Soil Vitality Max, Hydro Balance Pro, Canopy Care Elite, Vigor Spray Max, Crop Aid Myco Spora 10x.
- Use real horticultural terms where they are true (endophytic mycorrhizal fungi, foliar treatment, chelating agent, biostimulant) and give intervals as numbers ("reapply every **14-21** days").
- No emoji. Bold only the product name or a key figure inside body copy.

## Colour

- Text is `blue-lobelia` on `white`, `mist` or `patrinia-wash` sections. Use `stone` only for secondary text at 18px and above.
- `jadestone` is the brand teal: CTA fills, form underlines, card titles, eyebrows. `new-green` is its near-twin; prefer `jadestone` in new work.
- The primary action is the 284deg gradient `midsummer-field` → `poisonous-ice-cream` with a `new-yellow` border. Every button hovers to the 284deg `sun` → `sky` gradient. Use these gradients only on buttons.
- `poisonous-ice-cream` is the accent for a phrase in a hero headline on dark photography and for highlights on `blue-lobelia`. Never set it, `new-yellow` or `midsummer-field` as text on white.
- Alternate sections between `white` and `patrinia-wash` (11% `patrinia`) with `space-60` vertical padding. `spinnaker` and `crystal-rapids` are decorative tints only.
- Each product line has an accent token (`product-root-ignite-bio`, `product-soil-vitality-max`, `product-hydro-balance-pro`, `product-canopy-care-elite`, `product-vigor-spray-max`, `product-crop-aid-myco-spora`) used for its "Product Overview" label and packaging band. Use a product's accent only on that product's page or card.
- **Contrast.** Several live pairs miss WCAG AA: white on `jadestone` (2.99:1), white on the primary gradient (2.04:1 and lower), `sage` on white (3.04:1), `stone` on white (4.29:1). They are kept exact as the site has them. In new work, put `blue-lobelia` labels on light fills, or keep white labels at 19px bold or larger on `jadestone`.

## Type

- Poppins for everything (Google Fonts, weights 300–700). Manrope 300 only for `fine-print` consent text. Roboto and Montserrat appear in a few Elementor widgets on the live site; don't carry them forward.
- Scale: `hero` 56/1.2 bold uppercase (40px mobile) → `section-title` 32/36 bold → `eyebrow` 24 regular uppercase → `hero-sub` 22 medium → `lede` 20 bold (18 mobile) → `body-lg` 16 → `body` 15/1.6 → `card-text` 14 → `fine-print` 12 Manrope light.
- Body weight is 400; headings are 700. Medium (500) is for subheads and button labels.

## Space, shape and depth

- Sections: `space-60` top and bottom, `space-20` side gutter; the first section under the hero takes `space-100` on top. Columns and cards sit `space-40` apart.
- Radii by role: `radius-4` images and fields, `radius-8` product cards, `radius-12` CTA buttons, `radius-15` feature cards and rounded containers, `radius-25` the form submit, `radius-pill` pill buttons.
- Shadows are soft and large: `shadow-card` → `shadow-card-hover` on feature cards, `shadow-float` under product cards, `shadow-glow` under a hovered pill. No borders on cards; use `hairline` only for dividers.
- Motion: colour transitions 300ms; the primary pill lifts 3px over 700ms; product cards flip right over 1000ms. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by dropping the lift and flip.
- Breakpoints follow Elementor: mobile below 768px, tablet 768–1024px, desktop from 1025px. Below 500px, button rows stack full width with `space-12` gaps.

## Imagery

- Real photography of lawns, gardens, roots and seedlings, plus product bottle shots on white. Hero photography carries a dark overlay so white type holds 4.5:1.
- Product imagery sits on the product's accent colour or on white, never on a gradient.

## Iconography

- Font Awesome solid icons, used sparingly: the trailing `fa-arrow-right` on primary pills and small benefit icons in feature tiles, filled `jadestone`. Icons are single-ink and never emoji.
